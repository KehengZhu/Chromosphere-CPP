1/*!
2 * @file single_fluid/mixture.hpp
3 * @brief RELEASE solver API: the single-fluid field-aligned equilibrium-mixture
4 * model with conserved state U = (rho, rho u, E).
5 * @ingroup release_solver
6 *
7 * single_fluid/mixture.hpp — the RELEASE solver: a single-fluid field-aligned
8 * equilibrium-mixture model.
9 *
10 * ## Physical model
11 *
12 * One fluid, one velocity, one temperature, on a prescribed flux tube of
13 * cross-section A(s) with A(s) B(s) = const:
14 *
15 * d_t(rho) + (1/A) d_s(A rho u) = 0
16 * d_t(rho u) + (1/A) d_s(A (rho u^2 + p)) = p d_s(ln A) - rho d_s(phi_g)
17 * d_t(E) + (1/A) d_s(A (E + p) u) = -(1/A) d_s(A q)
18 *
19 * with the total energy, the equilibrium closure, and the conductive flux
20 *
21 * E = e_int(rho,T) + 1/2 rho u^2 + rho phi_g
22 * e_int = 3/2 p + x n_H chi_H, p = (1 + x) n_H k_B T, n_H = rho / m_H
23 * x = x_Saha(n_H, T) (pure-hydrogen Saha equilibrium)
24 * q = -kappa(n_e, n_HI, T) d_s T
25 * c_s = sqrt(Gamma1(T, n_H) p / rho) (CRASH Gamma1 table)
26 *
27 * The gravitational potential is carried inside E, so `phi_g` appears in the
28 * decode of every cell, ghost and reconstructed face.
29 *
30 * ## Software contract
31 *
32 * The conserved state is EXACTLY the three rows of `mix::` — there is no
33 * carrier-row storage, no equilibrium projection stage, and no electron
34 * compatibility row. The ionization fraction x, the carrier densities
35 * rho_i = x rho and rho_n = (1-x) rho, n_e and n_HI, and the electron partial
36 * pressure p_e are DERIVED diagnostics of (rho, e_int) produced by the EOS.
37 *
38 * The release timestep is exactly
39 *
40 * U^n -> MUSCL/Roe hydro -> U* -> implicit physical conduction -> U^{n+1}
41 *
42 * and nothing else. TRAC, nonthermal beam heating, volumetric coronal heating,
43 * radiative cooling and artificial/numerical conduction are NOT release physics;
44 * they exist only in the historical two-fluid research solver.
45 *
46 * The historical two-fluid solver lives in `two_fluid/two_fluid.hpp` and owns
47 * its own, separate seven-row state. Nothing here includes it, calls it, or
48 * depends on it; the only shared code is the Grid in `chromosphere.hpp` and the
49 * equilibrium EOS in `eos.hpp`.
50 */

92/// Cell-centre gravitational potential, the gauge every decode uses.
93inline double mixture_cell_phi(const Grid& grid, arma::uword i) {
94 return 0.5 * (static_cast<double>(grid.phi_g_imh(i))
95 + static_cast<double>(grid.phi_g_iph(i)));

121/// Nonlinear backward-Euler PHYSICAL conduction on the mixture energy row:
122/// Spitzer electron conduction plus neutral-hydrogen conduction, with no TRAC
123/// broadening and no artificial/numerical diffusivity anywhere in the operator.
124/// Mass and momentum are untouched; the accepted energy is the flux-updated
125/// internal energy plus the unchanged kinetic and gravitational parts, so the
126/// stage is conservative by construction. The outer thermal datum, when a
127/// scenario imposes one, sits at the PHYSICAL boundary face, half a top cell
128/// from the top cell centre — not at a ghost centre.
129Vec mixture_apply_conduction(const Grid& grid, const Vec& state, double dt);
