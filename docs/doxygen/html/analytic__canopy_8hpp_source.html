1/*!
2 * @file scenarios/analytic_canopy.hpp
3 * @brief Exponential magnetic-canopy B(z) overlay on the C7 thermodynamic profile.
4 * @ingroup scenarios
5 *
6 * Analytic canopy scenario.
7 *
8 * C7 thermodynamic profile (T, n_e, n_n) overlaid with the consensus
9 * exponential-canopy magnetic-field recipe
10 *
11 * B(z) = B_inf + (B_0 - B_inf) * exp(-(z - z_base) / H_B)
12 *
13 * Defaults (see Bellot Rubio & Orozco Suárez 2019; Kontar et al. 2008;
14 * Martínez-Sykora et al. 2019):
15 * B_0 = 100 G = 1.0e-2 T (footpoint network concentration)
16 * B_inf = 15 G = 1.5e-3 T (canopy-merged chromospheric value)
17 * H_B = 300 km (chromospheric magnetic scale height)
18 *
19 * Domain and IC otherwise identical to model_c7, so this scenario differs
20 * from model_c7 only through the ∂(1/B)/∂s source in flux.cpp:74.
21 */
