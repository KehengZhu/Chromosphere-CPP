Detailed Description

The production path: a single-fluid field-aligned equilibrium-mixture model whose conserved state is exactly U = (rho, rho u, E).

One fluid, one velocity, one temperature, on a prescribed flux tube. The whole timestep is

*     U^n -> MUSCL/Hancock/Roe hydro -> U* -> implicit physical conduction -> U^{n+1}
*

and contains no other stage: no equilibrium projection, no carrier-row storage, no TRAC, no beam heating, no volumetric coronal heating, no radiative cooling, and no artificial or numerical conduction.

Selected by loading a Gamma1 table, which the model_column scenario does unconditionally. See Numerical method for the discretization and Physical model for the equations.

◆ mixture_apply_conduction()

Vec chromosphere::mixture_apply_conduction ( const Grid & grid,
const Vec & state,
double dt )

Nonlinear backward-Euler PHYSICAL conduction on the mixture energy row: Spitzer electron conduction plus neutral-hydrogen conduction, with no TRAC broadening and no artificial/numerical diffusivity anywhere in the operator.

Mass and momentum are untouched; the accepted energy is the flux-updated internal energy plus the unchanged kinetic and gravitational parts, so the stage is conservative by construction. The outer thermal datum, when a scenario imposes one, sits at the PHYSICAL boundary face, half a top cell from the top cell centre — not at a ghost centre.
