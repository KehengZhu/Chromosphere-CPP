1/*!
2 * @file scenarios/model_flare.hpp
3 * @brief Explosive chromospheric evaporation scenario (nonthermal electron beam).
4 * @ingroup scenarios
5 *
6 * Explosive chromospheric evaporation scenario (Fisher, Canfield & McClymont
7 * 1985, ApJ 289, 425).
8 *
9 * Reuses the Model C7 chromosphere column (`model_c7_ic`) and its boundary
10 * closures (`model_c7_update_bc`), then adds a transient nonthermal-electron-
11 * beam volumetric heating source in the upper chromosphere and opens the outer
12 * boundary so the evaporated plasma can flow out supersonically. With a beam
13 * flux above the Fisher threshold (F_crit ≈ 7×10⁶ W m⁻²) the upper chromosphere
14 * cannot radiate the deposited energy, is heated to coronal temperatures, and
15 * drives an explosive upflow into the corona with a condensation downflow below.
16 *
17 * See docs/explosive_evaporation_plan.md.
18 */
