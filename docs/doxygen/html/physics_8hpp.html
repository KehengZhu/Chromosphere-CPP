Detailed Description

Inline physics formula library: collision frequencies, conductivities, radiative losses, heating rates, TRAC, and hydrogen ionization / recombination rate coefficients.

Inline physics helpers — collision frequencies, heat conductivities, and hydrogen ionization / recombination rate coefficients.

kappa_e / kappa_n are pure formulas in their inputs (no Grid dependency). nu_in needs k_b and m_i so it takes the Grid; the ionization/recombination rates take the Grid for χ_H and k_b.
